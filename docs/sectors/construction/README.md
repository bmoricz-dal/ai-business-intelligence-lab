# UK construction SMEs: AI adoption programme, 2026

Status: owner-approved publication
Prepared: 2026-08-11
Research mode: secondary data only

This package applies the accounting programme structure to UK construction SMEs while changing the workflow, professional context and evidence boundary.

## Connected outputs

1. **Readiness:** a direct SIC F current-state baseline using the ONS registered-enterprise frame and UKBDS 2026 sector estimates.
2. **Benefits and system fit:** a bounded review focused on document-heavy pre-construction and project-control work. It does not publish a construction-SME ROI.
3. **Adoption journeys:** a UK construction SME case, a larger UK construction-company action-research case and a research prototype, each kept separate.
4. **Construction Tender Lab:** a deterministic browser-only simulation for a fictional 18-person contractor and fictional tender pack.

## Publication boundary

The research director approved the sector selection, claim wording, source grading, fictional case and simulator assumptions on 11 August 2026. Publication must preserve the evidence boundaries below.

No page:

- multiplies UKBDS percentages by the ONS SME count;
- treats global RICS results as UK SME estimates;
- claims a sector productivity or ROI effect;
- generates a real bid, price, programme, method statement, safety assessment or regulatory submission; or
- accepts document uploads or transmits entered data.
