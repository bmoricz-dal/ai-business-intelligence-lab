# Construction AI benefits and system-fit review

Status: owner-review draft

## Question

For which construction workflows does current secondary evidence support a bounded AI test, what outcome can be measured locally, and which controls must remain in the workflow?

## Evidence decision

Evidence is strongest for **testability**, not for a sector effect. RICS respondents see potential in scheduling, progress monitoring, resource optimisation, document review and risk management; DSIT identifies tender preparation and programme coordination as practical training priorities; a 2026 research prototype reports strong requirement-extraction performance; and UK cases show use-case-led adoption. None establishes a representative UK construction-SME productivity, quality, safety or ROI effect.

## Publication boundary

- Lead with document-heavy pre-construction and tender support.
- Separate measured model performance, self-reported organisational outcomes, perceived potential and normative guidance.
- Require a firm-specific baseline covering preparation, checking, corrections, omissions, review and incidents.
- Do not allow AI output to become a final price, programme, safety judgement, design conclusion, contractual interpretation or regulatory submission.
