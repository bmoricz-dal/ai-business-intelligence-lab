# UK construction SMEs: AI adoption and operational readiness, 2026

Status: owner-approved publication
Prepared: 2026-08-11
Owner: research director / product owner
Research mode: secondary data only

## Objective

Produce a denominator-safe current-state study for UK construction SMEs covering AI use, system integration, policy/guidance, likely workflow entry points and implementation conditions.

## Scope

- UK enterprises classified to SIC 2007 section F: divisions 41, 42 and 43.
- SMEs with 0-249 employees in the ONS VAT/PAYE-registered enterprise frame.
- Construction contractors, specialist trades and civil-engineering businesses are in scope. Built-environment professional services outside SIC F are transfer evidence only.
- Secondary public or free-to-read evidence only.

## Research question

What does the best available secondary evidence show about the extent and operational depth of AI adoption among UK construction SMEs, and which bounded workflow is most suitable for an evidence-informed adoption test?

## Evidence decision

The ONS provides a direct SME population frame and UKBDS 2026 provides direct SIC F estimates, but UKBDS does not publish sector-by-size values. RICS supplies direct construction-professional evidence but is global and not SME-specific. DSIT's construction upskilling assessment and government-supported cases support workflow and implementation interpretation, not population prevalence.

The evidence is sufficient for a current-state page and bounded implementation prototype. It is insufficient for a construction-SME impact estimate, ROI benchmark, vendor ranking or claim that AI improves safety or regulatory compliance.

## Deliverables

- Sector prioritisation record and decision log.
- Current-state, benefits/system-fit and adoption-journey pages.
- Public evidence CSVs with denominators and claim limits.
- Deterministic Construction Tender Lab and step-level playbook.
- Automated route, content and interaction-boundary tests.
