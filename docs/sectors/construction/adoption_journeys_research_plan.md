# Construction AI adoption journeys

Status: owner-review draft

## Question

What do published construction cases reveal about the path from problem selection through pilot, setback, adaptation, control and outcome?

## Included evidence bundles

1. **Cast Consultancy:** named UK construction consultancy SME, around 90 employees; specific Gateway 2 workflow, tool-based training, live feedback and qualitative capability outcomes.
2. **ConstructionCo:** anonymised large UK housebuilder in BridgeAI-supported action research; earlier failed system, fragmented experimentation, requirements prioritisation, cross-functional working and a departmental document-review pilot.
3. **Bid-preparation research prototype:** two case organisations and a sample RFP demonstration; narrow extraction/retrieval performance, not a firm-level commercial outcome.

Cases are not blended into one journey. The first is the closest SME transfer case, but it is a built-environment consultancy rather than a typical SIC F contractor. The second is not an SME. The third is research-transfer evidence and does not establish UK context in the accessible record.
