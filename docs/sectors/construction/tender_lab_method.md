# Construction Tender Lab method

Status: owner-review draft

The lab is a deterministic fictional demonstration for **Northstar Build Ltd**, an 18-person UK contractor, responding to a fictional community-centre refurbishment tender. No AI model is called.

## Six workstations

1. Opportunity gate - decide whether the pack, deadline and capability justify review.
2. Requirement extraction - convert synthetic tender clauses into a compliance checklist.
3. Evidence matching - link approved fictional policies and case studies to response needs.
4. Draft control - distinguish source-backed draft material from missing or invented support.
5. Commercial and delivery challenge - surface assumptions, exclusions and review owners without calculating a bid price.
6. Submission assurance - require human sign-off, unresolved-item review and an audit trail.

## Boundary

Displayed completeness, time and control values are deterministic scenario mechanics. They are not observed outcomes, predictions or benchmarks. The lab does not create a real bid, price, programme, method statement, safety plan, contractual interpretation or regulatory submission. It accepts no uploads and sends no user input to DAL.
