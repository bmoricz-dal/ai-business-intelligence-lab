# Next-sector prioritisation, 2026

Status: owner-approved decision
Prepared: 2026-08-11
Decision owner: research director / product owner
Research mode: secondary data only

## Decision question

Which sector should follow accounting if the next programme must support the same chain from a denominator-safe current-state page through benefits, implementation journeys and a browser-only simulator?

## Recommendation

Prioritise **UK construction SMEs (SIC 2007 section F)**. Manufacturing is the runner-up.

This is a portfolio and research-priority recommendation, not evidence of market demand. It uses five criteria: registered-SME reach (25%), size of the adoption/readiness problem (25%), direct and authoritative evidence coverage (20%), suitability for a bounded simulator (20%), and portfolio distinctiveness from accounting (10%). Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5. The weights and scores are judgement inputs and should be approved or changed by the research director.

## Why construction leads

1. **Reach:** the ONS frame contains 384,525 registered construction SMEs, materially more than the other shortlisted definitions.
2. **Decision gap:** the official UKBDS construction estimates show reported use ahead of integration and formal/informal guidance. The supplied 95% intervals are wide - 14.2%-28.8% for any listed use, 1.9%-26.2% for integration and 0%-9.2% for policy/guidance - so the programme must show uncertainty rather than rank sectors by small differences.
3. **Direct evidence:** DSIT's 2026 upskilling research contains a dedicated construction assessment; RICS provides a large direct professional survey and a mandatory professional standard for RICS members and regulated firms; government-supported cases document implementation in a UK construction SME and a large housebuilder.
4. **Practical test:** tender preparation is explicitly named in DSIT's construction training priorities and is document-heavy, reviewable and reversible. A simulator can show requirement extraction, evidence matching, exception handling and human approval without producing engineering designs, safety decisions or real bids.
5. **Portfolio breadth:** construction introduces project delivery, subcontracting, site/commercial interfaces and safety-critical boundaries that are materially different from the accounting cycle.

## Sensitivity and limitations

- The ranking is not a statistical model and does not measure willingness to pay.
- UKBDS sector estimates are not sector-by-size estimates. They cannot be applied to the ONS SME counts to calculate numbers of adopting SMEs.
- RICS results are global, although 48% of the 2,200-plus Q1 2025 respondents were from the UK. UK-only and SME-only estimates are not published in the report.
- Technology and legal use broader UKBDS proxies, reducing their comparability grade.
- A sensitivity that increases authoritative evidence coverage to 40% and reduces reach and problem-gap weights to 15% each still leaves construction marginally ahead of manufacturing (4.60 versus 4.50), using the recorded judgement scores.
- The research director approved construction, the scoring method, the public wording and the fictional tender scenario on 11 August 2026.
