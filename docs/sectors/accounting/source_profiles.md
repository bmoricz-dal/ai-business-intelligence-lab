# Source profiles: UK accounting SME AI readiness

Prepared: 2026-08-01  
Status: final

## ACC-S004 - ONS UK business: activity, size and location 2025

- Role: direct accounting-sector population frame.
- Population: VAT and/or PAYE-registered UK enterprises.
- Reference date: 14 March 2025.
- Relevant table: Table 4, SIC 6920 by employment size.
- Key result: 39,860 enterprises with 0-249 employees; 115 with 250+; 39,975 total.
- Controls: counts are control-rounded to base 5; unregistered practices are excluded.
- Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0.

## ACC-S006 - Sage, Demos and ACCA Going for Growth 2024

- Role: direct accounting-practice adoption, technology and skills evidence.
- Method: online survey of 1,126 decision-makers at UK accounting and bookkeeping practices, 3-8 April 2024.
- Weighting: reported as weighted using ONS information on practice number, turnover and size.
- Key results used: 26% adopted; 54% piloting or adopted; leading technologies and skills-preparedness measures.
- Limits: includes large practices; raw microdata and full questionnaire not located; commercial sponsor; question-level bases are not separately published for every chart.
- Access: free-to-read PDF; copyright retained.

## ACC-S007 - AccountingWEB and Sage State of the Nation 2026

- Role: timely directional evidence on tools, tasks, integration preferences and barriers.
- Method: survey of more than 600 AccountingWEB members in practice and business.
- Key results used: 71.38% external non-embedded tools; 66.18% perceived embedded AI; task and barrier percentages reported in published follow-up analysis.
- Limits: self-selected community sample; mixes practice and business respondents; no representative SME population claim; exact question bases, fieldwork dates and weighting are not fully available publicly.
- Access: report/analysis pages are free or free with registration.

## ACC-S001 - DSIT UK Business Data Survey 2026

- Role: official broad-sector contextual proxy across the five dimensions.
- Fieldwork: October 2025 to January 2026.
- Relevant category: Professional, Scientific and Technical activities (SIC M).
- Key results used: any listed AI use, research, summarising/drafting, integration, policy/guidance, automated decisions, and AI development/training.
- Strengths: published confidence intervals and rounded unweighted bases; Open Government Licence.
- Limits: SIC M is much broader than accounting and published sector results are not SME-only; some measures use the conditional AI-user denominator.

## ACC-S003 - DSIT AI Adoption Research 2026

- Role: official economy-wide context for AI type, frequency, oversight and barriers.
- Method: 3,500 UK private-sector businesses with at least five employees; fieldwork 12 February to 2 May 2025; weighted by size and sector.
- Key contextual results: 16% currently use AI, 5% plan to use; NLP/text tools dominate among AI users, while agentic AI remains uncommon.
- Limits: excludes businesses with 0-4 employees and groups accounting inside a broad Business Services/Administration category.

## ACC-S008 and ACC-S009 - FRC and ICAEW guidance

- Role: normative governance and professional-practice context.
- Use: identify relevant controls such as confidentiality, human judgement, assurance and accountability.
- Limit: guidance does not measure adoption or prevalence and is never treated as survey evidence.
