# Accounting AI Adoption Journeys — decision log

## AJ-D01 — Secondary-data-only boundary

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** Use only published secondary evidence. Conduct no DAL survey, interview or primary case observation.
- **Reason:** Research-director instruction and comparability with the wider platform.

## AJ-D02 — Third accounting-sector output

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** Add `Accounting AI Adoption Journeys` under Sectors > Accounting. Keep the Accounting AI Experience Lab under AI in practice.
- **Reason:** The journey study explains real implementation evidence; the Lab remains the controlled practical demonstration.

## AJ-D03 — No synthetic firm histories

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** A case bundle can synthesise recurring mechanisms but may not merge unrelated organisations into one apparently real journey.
- **Reason:** Triangulation increases interpretive confidence but cannot create missing longitudinal events.

## AJ-D04 — Outcome-status labels

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** Mark every outcome as experimental, associated, qualitative, self-reported, anticipated, transfer-only or comparator-only.
- **Reason:** The sources use incompatible designs and cannot be pooled.

## AJ-D05 — Comparator separation

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** Alfa/Beta and Hudson remain historical non-AI automation comparators; Deloitte, KPMG and PwC remain non-practice transfer cases.
- **Reason:** Their implementation lessons are useful, but relabelling them as accounting-practice AI evidence would overstate scope.

## AJ-D06 — Publication gate

- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Decision:** Complete and test locally, then request a separate research-director publication approval before GitHub push or Cloudflare deployment.
- **Reason:** The page introduces new public research claims and source interpretations.
