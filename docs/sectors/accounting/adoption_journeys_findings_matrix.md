# Accounting AI Adoption Journeys — findings matrix

## Publishable cross-case findings

| ID | Finding | Evidence | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| AJ-F01 | Adoption works best as a bounded workflow redesign rather than an unstructured tool rollout. | AJ01, AJ02, AJ03, AJ04, AJ12-AJ14 | Recurring implementation pattern, not a measured success probability. |
| AJ-F02 | Data and process preparation precede dependable AI performance. | AJ01, AJ10-AJ14 | Historical and finance-function cases corroborate the mechanism but are not accounting-practice AI outcomes. |
| AJ-F03 | Human review moves to exceptions, confidence checks and final accountability; it does not disappear. | AJ01, AJ02, AJ07, AJ09, AJ12 | Strongest direct evidence is task-specific; control requirements vary by engagement. |
| AJ-F04 | Training, client readiness, communication and organisational capacity affect whether a pilot scales. | AJ03-AJ07, AJ10, AJ11 | Mainly qualitative and historical evidence; not a quantified SME effect. |
| AJ-F05 | Benefits and errors can coexist. | AJ01, AJ02, AJ07 | The only causal estimate is a narrow classification experiment; LYA savings are self-reported. |
| AJ-F06 | Work may shift from routine processing toward review, explanation and advisory contact. | AJ01, AJ06, AJ10, AJ11 | Associated or comparator evidence, not a deterministic workforce forecast. |

## Evidence gaps

- No open longitudinal case follows a named UK micro accounting practice from AI baseline through measured scaled outcomes.
- No source supplies a complete independent before-and-after cost, quality, correction, training and governance ledger.
- Client-business effects are largely absent from the accounting-practice evidence.
- Vendor case studies rarely provide counterfactuals, complete denominators or independent validation.
- Audit-firm evidence is richer than tax, bookkeeping and accounts-preparation evidence but transfers imperfectly to micro practices.

## Concluding insight

The evidence supports a practical sequence—define a workflow, prepare data and controls, pilot with human review, measure exceptions and quality, then scale conditionally. It does not support a general claim that adopting a particular product guarantees time savings, ROI, fewer staff or better client outcomes.
