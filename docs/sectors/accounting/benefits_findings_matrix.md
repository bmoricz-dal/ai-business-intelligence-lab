## Concluding assessment

The strongest current secondary evidence supports a **controlled augmentation** pathway, not autonomous accounting. Bookkeeping, transaction categorisation, reconciliation and month-end close have the clearest measurable signal because the system can combine structured accounting data, workflow rules, confidence scores and human review. Evidence for drafting, research and client communication is plausible and consistent with wider experimental literature, but accounting-specific outcome measurement is thinner.

For UK accounting SMEs, the appropriate claim is that measurable benefits are plausible and have been observed in a relevant international field setting, while UK firms also report productivity and process benefits in broad official research. The evidence does not yet quantify a causal UK accounting-SME productivity uplift or ROI.

## System-fit conclusion

| Adoption position | Suitable workflows | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Controlled deployment | Transaction coding, reconciliations, close checklists, draft explanations and internal research | Approved data, workflow rules, confidence or exception handling, documented human review |
| Conditional deployment | Client communications, advisory preparation, forecasting and contract or document review | Qualified review, source checking, escalation and testing against known cases |
| Do not delegate autonomously | Tax conclusions, audit conclusions, material postings and decisions with financial or regulatory consequences | Professional judgement and accountability must remain with a qualified person |

## Prohibited interpretations

- Do not describe the 7.5 to 7.9 day estimate as a UK SME benchmark.
- Do not treat ledger granularity as a direct error-rate or audit-quality measure.
- Do not present self-reported UK productivity percentages as measured causal effects.
- Do not infer vendor superiority from a single partner-platform study.
- Do not produce a composite benefits score, ROI estimate or autonomous-accounting recommendation.
