## Approved concluding statement

AI has moved beyond niche experimentation in UK accounting, but the measured level of adoption changes with the definition used. Current evidence is most consistent with task-level, language and information assistance rather than sector-wide workflow transformation. External tools and vendor-embedded features dominate; secure integration, governance, human review and workforce capability remain separate operational challenges. No claim is made about business benefits, ROI or the effectiveness of individual systems.

## Prohibited interpretations

- Do not call 26%, 54%, 71.38% or 50.6% “the” accounting-SME adoption rate.
- Do not average those figures or present them as a trend.
- Do not infer active use merely from perceived embedded AI.
- Do not add multiple-response use-case percentages.
- Do not infer benefits, causality or readiness scores.
