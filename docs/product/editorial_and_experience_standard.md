# DAL Editorial and Experience Standard

## Objective

Make DAL feel like a professional intelligence product: answer-led, evidence-bounded, visually distinctive and useful at the point of decision. The standard applies to website pages, interactive exhibits and downloadable publications.

It is informed by representative official BCG and McKinsey pages, but it does not copy their branding, layouts, wording, statistics or proprietary frameworks.

## Benchmark reviewed

### BCG

- [Artificial Intelligence at Scale](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence): outcome-led capability framing, operating plays, service architecture and client-impact modules.
- [Artificial Intelligence insights](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/insights): featured insight hierarchy, format labels, page outline and mixed editorial media.
- [About BCG](https://www.bcg.com/about/overview) and [Purpose and people](https://www.bcg.com/about/purpose-people): purpose, values and behaviour-led principles.
- Representative publication pages: early key takeaways, content type/date/read-time metadata, exhibits, leader questions and download actions.

### McKinsey

- [QuantumBlack approach](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-approach): human and technical capability presented as one operating system.
- [The state of AI: How organizations are rewiring to capture value](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-how-organizations-are-rewiring-to-capture-value): answer-led headline, workflow redesign, governance, organisational implications and transparent analytical limits.
- [The big rethink](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-big-rethink-an-agenda-for-thriving-in-the-agentic-age): shift from tool deployment to end-to-end workflow redesign.
- [Year in visuals](https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/year-in-review/year-in-visuals) and [Year in charts](https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/year-in-review/year-in-charts): purposeful motion, layered visual texture and single-message exhibits.
- [McKinsey purpose, mission and values](https://www.mckinsey.com/about-us/overview/our-purpose-mission-and-values): clear purpose, professional standards and behaviour-led values.

## DAL editorial method

1. Lead with the answer or tension, not the report label.
2. Put the evidence statement, decision implication and claim boundary next to one another.
3. Use one message per exhibit. Highlight the decision-relevant signal and mute context.
4. State the population, denominator, date and limitation close to every quantitative finding.
5. End major sections with the decision they clarify, not a generic summary.
6. Keep practical recommendations labelled as implications, design principles, hypotheses or scenario choices unless a source directly establishes an effect.
7. Treat missing evidence as a finding boundary, never as permission to estimate.

## DAL visual system

- Deep navy editorial fields, white space and restrained cyan/emerald/violet signal colours.
- Two original signature motifs: the evidence sphere for traceable signals and route lines for movement from national context to workflow decisions.
- Cinematic media has a narrative job: the aerial London sequence represents the UK business system; the office sequence represents firm-level workflow adoption.
- Motion is slow, directional and sparse. It supports hierarchy and continuity rather than decorating every object.
- Cards and controls respond to hover, focus and selection with position, light and border changes. Every control remains keyboard operable.
- The fixed page outline, reading-progress indicator and section reveal system help readers navigate long research pages.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable continuous motion and reveal transitions.

## Publication standard

- Answer-led cover headline with formal report title as secondary metadata.
- Executive brief on page two.
- Consistent folio, report identity, evidence note and page numbering.
- Strong visual separation between finding, implication and limitation.
- Charts retain official estimates, intervals, denominators and benchmark labels.
- Source trail, research boundary and citation remain part of the publication, not supplementary decoration.

## Quality boundary

BCG and McKinsey operate with large editorial, design, research and engineering teams. DAL can adopt comparable disciplines—clarity, hierarchy, interaction quality, evidence control and editorial consistency—without claiming organisational equivalence or imitating protected brand expression.
