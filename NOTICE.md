## Official statistics

The source data are from the Department for Science, Innovation and
Technology's UK Business Data Survey 2026.

- Publication:
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/uk-business-data-survey-2026>
- Publisher: Department for Science, Innovation and Technology
- Publication date: 18 June 2026
- Fieldwork: 10 October 2025 to 28 January 2026
- Stated licence: Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated

Raw source workbooks are not redistributed in this repository. The included
download helper retrieves them from their official URLs and checks their
registered SHA-256 fingerprints.
