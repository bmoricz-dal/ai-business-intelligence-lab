# Public evidence extracts

These CSV files contain the observations used in the first five reports.

- `report_01_ai_use_by_size.csv`
- `report_02_ai_integration_by_size.csv`
- `report_03_ai_governance_by_size.csv`
- `report_04_ai_use_cases_by_size.csv`
- `report_05_adoption_pathways_by_size.csv`

Estimates are stored as proportions. Confidence limits are the supplied 95%
limits. Sample bases are rounded unweighted respondent bases, not counts of UK
businesses.

The source is the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology's UK
Business Data Survey 2026.

## Construction sector programme

- `construction_ai_readiness_2026.csv`
- `construction_ai_benefits_system_fit_2026.csv`
- `construction_ai_adoption_journeys_2026.csv`
- `construction_tender_ai_adoption_playbook_2026.csv`

The construction extracts keep official sector estimates, industry evidence,
implementation cases and fictional simulator steps separate. UKBDS sector
estimates are not construction-SME-only estimates.
